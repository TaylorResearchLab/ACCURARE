## Original scientific aims and disease context

The funded proposal sought to identify genomic features underlying observed co-occurrence of **childhood cancers (CCs)** and **structural birth defects (SBDs)**. It proposed to integrate germline and tumor data from the **Kids First** resource with childhood cancer genomics from the **NCI Molecular Targets Project**, using the Common Fund Data Ecosystem (CFDE) **Data Distillery Knowledge Graph (DDKG)**.

The selected biological comparisons included:
- Congenital heart defects with neuroblastoma or hematological malignancies.
- Congenital brain/CNS defects with brain tumors.

The proposal described expansion of the DDKG schema to accommodate additional genomic data types, evidence-weighted relationships, and scientific analysis by:
- Topological link prediction and the Connect the Dots method.
- Graph embeddings, dimensionality reduction, and cluster detection.
- Graph neural networks for heterogeneous graphs, including PyTorch Geometric.
- Methods to make evidence and analysis outputs accessible to the broader research community.

These are proposal objectives stated in the award abstract. Individual scientific outcomes and completed deliverables require their own evidence, versioned analyses, or publications.

## Relationship to ACCURARE

**ACCURARE** is an emerging public scientific resource that builds on the U24's childhood cancer and structural birth defect research foundation.

Its broader direction includes:
- Pediatric cancer genomics and expression from OpenPedCan and relevant original resources.
- Human embryonic and fetal expression and spatial references.
- Nonhuman primate developmental expression and cross-species comparisons.
- Pediatric tissue references from dGTEx.
- Curated rare disease, developmental disorder, and phenotype evidence.
- A public discovery portal and a proposed researcher-driven AI Hypothesis Studio using a skill and authenticated MCP evidence access.

The U24 award should be credited for its scientific foundation. The later extensions should carry their own dataset, licensing, method, personnel, and funding attribution. Data sources are incorporated only under their applicable use and publication terms.
